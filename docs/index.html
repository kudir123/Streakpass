What is StreakPass?

StreakPass is a small on-chain ritual on BNB Chain. Your Agent ID is the key: if your wallet holds one, you can check in once per UTC day. Check in on consecutive days to build a streak; miss a day and the running streak restarts (your best streak is kept). Reaching 3, 7, 14 or 30 days mints a soulbound badge — it lives in your wallet forever and can never be sold, traded or transferred.

The contract has no owner and no admin functions: nobody can mint badges by hand, pause the passport, or touch anyone's streak. The only way in is holding an Agent ID and showing up.

Risk note: StreakPass badges are non-transferable collectibles with no monetary value. StreakPass is not a financial product, promises no returns, and nothing here is financial advice. Cryptocurrency activity always carries risk — never spend more than you can afford to lose.
